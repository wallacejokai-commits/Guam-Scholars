Programs

Local, regional, national, and international apprenticeship, mentorship, and enrichment programs open to Guam high school students. Last reviewed: August 15, 2026

Before You Apply

Always confirm eligibility, session dates, and application requirements directly on the program's official page. Some programs offer travel stipends or are fully virtual, which matters for students applying from Guam.

Local (Guam)

  • GTA Youth Leader Program: Youth leadership and service-recognition program with academic scholarships and other prizes; eligibility: Guam residents enrolled in a local high school for SY 2026–27, with a 3.0+ cumulative GPA, leadership experience, and a recommendation; deadline: August 30, 2026.
  • Governor’s Summer Youth Employment Program (GSYEP): Paid summer work experience through the Guam Department of Youth Affairs; eligibility: Guam residents ages 15–17 for the 2026 childcare component; application window: announced annually in spring.
  • Governor’s Youth Advisory Council: Youth leadership body advising Guam’s Governor and Lt. Governor on student and youth issues; eligibility: student leaders from Guam high schools, colleges, and universities; application window: announced by the Office of the Governor.
  • Guam Department of Labor Youth Program / Passport to Careers: Career exploration, work experience, mentoring, internships, and training. The Year-Round In-School Youth Employment pathway provides eligible 11th- and 12th-grade students ages 16–18 with career-readiness training and paid summer work experience; broader Youth Program eligibility includes published income and employment-barrier criteria. Application window: year-round through the American Job Center.
  • Guam Green Growth Youth Conservation Corps (via GSYEP): Paid hands-on sustainability, conservation, agriculture, aquaculture, and renewable-energy experience through the Governor’s Summer Youth Employment Program; eligibility: Guam high school students selected through GSYEP; application window: apply to GSYEP when the annual summer cycle opens.
  • Junior Achievement Company Program: Entrepreneurship program where students run their own business; eligibility: Guam high school students (Grades 9-12); application window: September - October.
  • UOG Advanced High School Program: Earn college credit while completing high school; eligibility: incoming Guam public, private, or charter-school seniors with a 3.0+ GPA, at least one year of Guam residency, counselor recommendation, and parent/guardian approval; deadline: August 7, 2026 for Fall 2026.
  • UOG TRiO Upward Bound: Free college-preparation program with tutoring, summer coursework, college/financial-aid support, and activities; eligibility: first-generation and/or low-income Guam high school students in grades 9–12 who meet GPA and citizenship/residency criteria; application window: annual. Confirm with UOG.
  • Guam Youth Congress: Civic-leadership body established under Guam law that gives young leaders a platform for civic participation, leadership development, and youth-policy work; eligibility: Guam youth and student representatives who meet the current selection requirements; application window: confirm the current representative-selection cycle directly with Guam Youth Congress.

Regional (Pacific/Asia)

  • Pacific STEP-UP Guam High School Research Program: Mentored summer research at UOG or another local agency, with an annual Guam Local High School Summer Research Symposium and a chance for two students to present at the National High School STEP-UP Scientific Research Symposium in Washington, D.C.; eligibility: confirm current Guam high school requirements with the program; application period: November 15–February 15 annually. A participation allowance is provided after successful completion.

National (U.S.)

  • Anson L. Clark Scholars Program: Summer research program at Texas Tech University; eligibility: US high school seniors/recent graduates (17+ years old); deadline: February 16, 2026.
  • MIT Beaver Works Summer Institute (BWSI): Project-based STEM program for high-achieving students entering senior year of high school; review the official program for current location, eligibility, and application details.
  • Caltech Up Close: A fall fly-in program for prospective students; eligibility: US high school seniors (class of 2027); deadline: August 9, 2026.
  • Explore Bowdoin: A fly-in program for students to visit Bowdoin College; eligibility: US high school seniors intending to enter college in Fall 2027; deadline: August 11.
  • FLEX Abroad: Exchange program for students to live and study in another country; eligibility: US citizens, high school students; application window: Fall.
  • Hutton Junior Fisheries Biology Program: Paid, eight-week fisheries and aquatic-science mentorship/internship with a $3,000 scholarship after successful completion; eligibility: high school students living in the U.S., Puerto Rico, Mexico, or Canada. Applicants must be matched with a mentor within about 30–45 minutes of home, so Guam mentor availability must be confirmed; application window: 2027 applications open in November 2026.
  • MITES (MIT Introduction to Technology, Engineering, and Science): Summer STEM program at MIT; eligibility: US high school juniors; deadline: February 1.
  • NASA STEM Enhancement in Earth Science (SEES): NASA-supported STEM research experience with virtual research, a science symposium, and selected on-site projects at the University of Texas Center for Space Research; eligibility: U.S.-citizen high school sophomores or juniors who are at least 16 by the program start date. The 2026 cycle is closed; monitor the next annual cycle. Some on-site projects may require travel, with limited travel scholarships potentially available.
  • NAVFAC Pathways Internship Program: Paid federal career-exploration internships offered through Navy Facilities Engineering Systems Command and USAJOBS; eligibility: current high school students (or students accepted at least part-time in a qualifying educational program), subject to each job announcement’s age, citizenship, location, and hiring requirements; application window: opportunities posted individually on USAJOBS.
  • NSLI-Y (National Security Language Initiative for Youth): Language immersion programs abroad; eligibility: US citizens, high school students, 15-18 years old; deadline: October/November.
  • Naval STEM Interns (SEAP): Eight-week, paid science and engineering apprenticeship at participating Department of Navy laboratories; eligibility: currently enrolled high school students who have completed grade 9, generally age 16+ by the internship start date, with U.S. citizenship required by most laboratories; application window: August 1–November 1 annually. Guam students may apply, but must meet a selected laboratory’s requirements and arrange housing.
  • PROMYS: Intensive math program at Boston University; eligibility: High school students (14-18 years old); deadline: February 27, 2026.
  • RISE (Research in Science and Engineering): Summer program at Boston University; eligibility: US high school seniors; deadline: February 4, 2026.
  • Research Science Institute (RSI): Intensive summer research program at MIT; eligibility: High school juniors; deadline: January.
  • SAMS (Summer Academy for Math and Science): Pre-college program at Carnegie Mellon; eligibility: US high school juniors (16+ years old) with financial need; deadline: February/March.
  • SSP (Summer Science Program): Residential summer research experience in astrophysics or biochemistry; eligibility: High school juniors; deadline: February.
  • Simons Summer Research Program: Hands-on research at Stony Brook University; eligibility: US high school juniors (16+ years old); deadline: February 5, 2026.
  • Stanford SIMR: Stanford Institutes of Medicine Summer Research Program; eligibility: US high school juniors/seniors (16+ years old); deadline: February.
  • Stanford SUMaC: Stanford University Mathematics Camp; eligibility: High school students (grades 10-11); deadline: February 2, 2026.
  • U.S. Service Academies: Federal undergraduate military academies; eligibility, nomination, and application requirements vary by academy, so review the official academy and applicable nomination process.
  • YES Abroad: Kennedy-Lugar Youth Exchange and Study program for study abroad; eligibility: US citizens, high school students; application window: Fall.
  • Yale Summer Program in Astrophysics (YSPA): Research-based summer program at Yale; eligibility: High school juniors; deadline: February.

International

  • United World Colleges (UWC) / UWC-USA: Two-year International Baccalaureate education at UWC campuses worldwide; eligibility: U.S. citizens or permanent residents, including qualifying Guam students, may use the U.S. application pathway. Students typically begin at age 16 or 17; confirm current age, selection, financial-assistance, and application-cycle requirements directly with UWC-USA.
  • Yale Young Global Scholars (YYGS): Summer enrichment program for students from around the world; eligibility: High school students (16-18 years old); deadline: January.
