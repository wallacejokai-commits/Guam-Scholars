Quick Fact

Every listing on this site is checked against one question: can a high school student physically located on Guam actually apply to this? If the answer is no, it doesn't go on the list.

Our Mission

Guam Scholars exists to make it easier for high school students on Guam to find scholarships, programs, and competitions they can actually apply to — whether they're based here on-island, elsewhere in the Pacific, in the mainland U.S., or open internationally. Distance from the mainland shouldn't mean missing out on opportunities other students hear about first. This site is meant to be concise and genuinely useful, not another link dump.

What This Site Covers

  • Scholarships — local, regional, national, and international opportunities open to Guam students.
  • Programs — apprenticeships, mentorships, and summer enrichment programs, including remote/virtual options that don't require travel off-island.
  • Competitions — academic contests students on Guam can register for and compete in, in person or remotely.
  • Standardized Testing — what the SAT, ACT, and ASVAB are, why they matter, and how to register for them from Guam.
  • College Applications — how to apply on-island (UOG, GCC) and off-island (Common App, FAFSA, and mainland U.S. schools).

Keeping It Current

Deadlines and eligibility rules change every year. We do our best to keep listings accurate, but you should always double-check details on the official scholarship, program, or testing organization's own website before applying — especially deadlines. If you spot something outdated, please let us know using the form at the bottom of any page.

Accessibility

We're working to meet WCAG accessibility guidelines so this site is usable for everyone, including students using screen readers, keyboard navigation, or other assistive technology. If you run into an accessibility barrier anywhere on this site, please tell us — we want to fix it.

Why I Built Guam Scholars

Throughout high school, I spent a lot of time searching for scholarships, programs, competitions, and other opportunities. I found some great ones, but I also learned about many valuable opportunities too late, often only after they had already passed or appeared in the news after the deadline. It was difficult to find one reliable place that collected information relevant to students on Guam.

I created Guam Scholars as the resource I wish I had when I was in high school: one place to find scholarships, programs, competitions, and other information that can be difficult to discover or easy to learn about too late. I hope it helps students find opportunities that genuinely interest them early enough to apply, feel less lost about where to look, and see that opportunities are everywhere, even for students on Guam.

I check listings against official provider pages and update them when I can, but students should always confirm current eligibility, deadlines, and requirements directly with the organization offering the opportunity. If you are a student, parent, or other visitor and notice something that needs updating, or know of an opportunity that should be included, please feel free to send me a message through the form on this site.

Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Guam Scholars is an independent educational resource for students, families, and other visitors looking for scholarships, programs, competitions, college information, and related opportunities. I respect your privacy and aim to collect as little information as possible.

Information you choose to send

You can use the contact form at the bottom of the site to suggest an opportunity, report an update, or send a question. When you submit the form, I receive the information you choose to provide, which may include your name, email address, and message. Name is optional. Email is required so I can reply when a response is appropriate.

Please do not send sensitive personal information through the form. This includes Social Security numbers, student identification numbers, account passwords, financial information, medical information, application documents, or anything else you would not want sent by ordinary email.

How I use form messages

I use messages only to review suggestions, correct information, consider opportunities for the site, and respond when appropriate. I may not be able to respond personally to every submission. Only I can access the messages I receive through the form. I keep messages only for as long as I decide they are needed to review, respond to, or address the submission, then I delete them.

Service providers and hosting

Guam Scholars is hosted through GitHub Pages. The contact form is processed by FormSubmit. These services may process information needed to host the site or deliver your form message under their own terms and privacy practices. This notice does not control their independent practices. You can review the GitHub Privacy Statement and FormSubmit Privacy Policy for more information.

I do not operate user accounts, analytics, social-media widgets, or embedded media on Guam Scholars. Guam Scholars does not currently use advertising or advertising trackers. If I add advertising or another service that changes how visitor information is handled, I will update this Privacy Notice before or when that change takes effect.

The site contains links to third-party organizations and resources. If you follow one of those links, that organization’s privacy practices apply to its own website.

Corrections and deletion requests

If you want me to correct or delete a message you previously sent through the form, please use the same website form. Include the email address you used and the approximate date of your submission so I can locate it. I will make a reasonable effort to address the request when I can.

Changes to this notice

I may update this notice as Guam Scholars changes. The date at the top of this section shows when it was last updated.
