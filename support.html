A Free Resource

Guam Scholars is independently created and maintained to help students find scholarships, programs, competitions, and college resources relevant to Guam.

Why Support Matters

Guam Scholars is a free, independent project. Keeping a resource website current takes time and has practical costs, including the domain name, website upkeep, and future improvements that make opportunities easier to find.

If this site has helped you, optional support can help keep it free to use and help it grow over time. No contribution is required to access any Guam Scholars content.

How Support Will Work

The support page will use Ko-fi, a third-party service. Guam Scholars will not collect or store payment-card, bank-account, or other payment details on this website. When the public support link is ready, selecting it will take visitors to Ko-fi to complete any voluntary contribution.

Support link coming soon: The Ko-fi page is being set up. Please check back soon for the optional support link.

Transparency

Guam Scholars is an independent personal project. Optional contributions may help cover the website domain, upkeep, and future improvements. Contributions are not currently represented as tax-deductible charitable donations.

Support is voluntary, and it does not buy placement, endorsements, special access, scholarship advice, or any guarantee of an outcome. Listings are selected based on their relevance and usefulness for students on Guam.

Other Ways to Help

You can help without contributing money. Share Guam Scholars with students, families, counselors, teachers, and community organizations. You can also use the contact form below to suggest a scholarship, program, competition, or correction that should be added.
