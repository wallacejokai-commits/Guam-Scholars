Quick Fact

Every listing on this site is checked against one question: can a high school student physically located on Guam actually apply to this? If the answer is no, it doesn't go on the list.

Our Mission

Guam Scholars exists to make it easier for high school students on Guam to find scholarships, programs, and competitions they can actually apply to — whether they're based here on-island, elsewhere in the Pacific, in the mainland U.S., or open internationally. Distance from the mainland shouldn't mean missing out on opportunities other students hear about first. This site is meant to be concise and genuinely useful, not another link dump.

What This Site Covers

  • Scholarships — local, regional, national, and international opportunities open to Guam students.
  • Programs — apprenticeships, mentorships, and summer enrichment programs, including remote/virtual options that don't require travel off-island.
  • Competitions — academic contests students on Guam can register for and compete in, in person or remotely.
  • Standardized Testing — what the SAT, ACT, and ASVAB are, why they matter, and how to register for them from Guam.
  • College Applications — how to apply on-island (UOG, GCC) and off-island (Common App, FAFSA, and mainland U.S. schools).

Keeping It Current

Deadlines and eligibility rules change every year. We do our best to keep listings accurate, but you should always double-check details on the official scholarship, program, or testing organization's own website before applying — especially deadlines. If you spot something outdated, please let us know using the form at the bottom of any page.

Accessibility

We're working to meet WCAG accessibility guidelines so this site is usable for everyone, including students using screen readers, keyboard navigation, or other assistive technology. If you run into an accessibility barrier anywhere on this site, please tell us — we want to fix it.

Disclaimer

Guam Scholars is an independent, informational resource. We are not affiliated with the Guam Department of Education, the University of Guam, Guam Community College, the College Board, ACT Inc., the U.S. Department of Defense, or any scholarship, program, or competition provider listed on this site, unless explicitly stated. Inclusion of a listing is not an endorsement, and we cannot guarantee eligibility, deadlines, or outcomes — always verify directly with the provider.
