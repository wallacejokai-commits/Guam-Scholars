Programs

Local, regional, national, and international apprenticeship, mentorship, and enrichment programs open to Guam high school students.

Before You Apply

Always confirm eligibility, session dates, and application requirements directly on the program's official page. Some programs offer travel stipends or are fully virtual, which matters for students applying from Guam.

Local (Guam)

  • GTA Youth Leader Program — Youth leadership and service-recognition program with academic scholarships and other prizes; eligibility: Guam residents enrolled in a local high school for SY 2026–27, with a 3.0+ cumulative GPA, leadership experience, and a recommendation; deadline: August 30, 2026. Learn more
  • Governor’s Summer Youth Employment Program (GSYEP) — Paid summer work experience through the Guam Department of Youth Affairs; eligibility: Guam residents ages 15–17 for the 2026 childcare component; application window: announced annually in spring. Learn more
  • Governor’s Youth Advisory Council — Youth leadership body advising Guam’s Governor and Lt. Governor on student and youth issues; eligibility: student leaders from Guam high schools, colleges, and universities; application window: announced by the Office of the Governor. Learn more
  • Guam Department of Labor Youth Program / Passport to Careers — Career exploration, work experience, mentoring, internships, and training; eligibility: Guam youth ages 14–24 who meet federal income and employment-barrier criteria; application window: year-round through the American Job Center. Learn more
  • Guam Green Growth Youth Conservation Corps (via GSYEP) — Paid hands-on sustainability, conservation, agriculture, aquaculture, and renewable-energy experience through the Governor’s Summer Youth Employment Program; eligibility: Guam high school students selected through GSYEP; application window: apply to GSYEP when the annual summer cycle opens. Learn more
  • Junior Achievement Company Program — Entrepreneurship program where students run their own business; eligibility: Guam high school students (Grades 9-12); application window: September - October. Learn more
  • UOG Advanced High School Program — Earn college credit while completing high school; eligibility: incoming Guam public, private, or charter-school seniors with a 3.0+ GPA, at least one year of Guam residency, counselor recommendation, and parent/guardian approval; deadline: August 7, 2026 for Fall 2026. Learn more
  • UOG TRiO Upward Bound — Free college-preparation program with tutoring, summer coursework, college/financial-aid support, and activities; eligibility: first-generation and/or low-income Guam high school students in grades 9–12 who meet GPA and citizenship/residency criteria; application window: annual—confirm with UOG. Learn more

National (U.S.)

  • Anson L. Clark Scholars Program — Summer research program at Texas Tech University; eligibility: US high school seniors/recent graduates (17+ years old); deadline: February 16, 2026. Learn more
  • MIT Beaver Works Summer Institute (BWSI) — Project-based STEM program for high-achieving students entering senior year of high school; review the official program for current location, eligibility, and application details. Learn more
  • Caltech Up Close — A fall fly-in program for prospective students; eligibility: US high school seniors (class of 2027); deadline: August 9, 2026. Learn more
  • Explore Bowdoin — A fly-in program for students to visit Bowdoin College; eligibility: US high school seniors intending to enter college in Fall 2027; deadline: August 11. Learn more
  • FLEX Abroad — Exchange program for students to live and study in another country; eligibility: US citizens, high school students; application window: Fall. Learn more
  • MITES (MIT Introduction to Technology, Engineering, and Science) — Summer STEM program at MIT; eligibility: US high school juniors; deadline: February 1. Learn more
  • NAVFAC Pathways Internship Program — Paid federal career-exploration internships offered through Navy Facilities Engineering Systems Command and USAJOBS; eligibility: current high school students (or students accepted at least part-time in a qualifying educational program), subject to each job announcement’s age, citizenship, location, and hiring requirements; application window: opportunities posted individually on USAJOBS. Learn more
  • NSLI-Y (National Security Language Initiative for Youth) — Language immersion programs abroad; eligibility: US citizens, high school students, 15-18 years old; deadline: October/November. Learn more
  • Naval STEM Interns (SEAP) — Eight-week, paid science and engineering apprenticeship at participating Department of Navy laboratories; eligibility: currently enrolled high school students who have completed grade 9, generally age 16+ by the internship start date, with U.S. citizenship required by most laboratories; application window: August 1–November 1 annually. Guam students may apply, but must meet a selected laboratory’s requirements and arrange housing. Learn more
  • PROMYS — Intensive math program at Boston University; eligibility: High school students (14-18 years old); deadline: February 27, 2026. Learn more
  • RISE (Research in Science and Engineering) — Summer program at Boston University; eligibility: US high school seniors; deadline: February 4, 2026. Learn more
  • Research Science Institute (RSI) — Intensive summer research program at MIT; eligibility: High school juniors; deadline: January. Learn more
  • SAMS (Summer Academy for Math and Science) — Pre-college program at Carnegie Mellon; eligibility: US high school juniors (16+ years old) with financial need; deadline: February/March. Learn more
  • SSP (Summer Science Program) — Residential summer research experience in astrophysics or biochemistry; eligibility: High school juniors; deadline: February. Learn more
  • Simons Summer Research Program — Hands-on research at Stony Brook University; eligibility: US high school juniors (16+ years old); deadline: February 5, 2026. Learn more
  • Stanford SIMR — Stanford Institutes of Medicine Summer Research Program; eligibility: US high school juniors/seniors (16+ years old); deadline: February. Learn more
  • Stanford SUMaC — Stanford University Mathematics Camp; eligibility: High school students (grades 10-11); deadline: February 2, 2026. Learn more
  • U.S. Service Academies — Federal undergraduate military academies; eligibility, nomination, and application requirements vary by academy, so review the official academy and applicable nomination process. Learn more
  • YES Abroad — Kennedy-Lugar Youth Exchange and Study program for study abroad; eligibility: US citizens, high school students; application window: Fall. Learn more
  • Yale Summer Program in Astrophysics (YSPA) — Research-based summer program at Yale; eligibility: High school juniors; deadline: February. Learn more

International
