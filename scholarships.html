Scholarships

Before You Apply

Always confirm eligibility, deadlines, and required documents directly on the scholarship's official page before applying. Guam students are U.S. citizens/nationals and qualify for most national scholarships open to U.S. territories unless otherwise noted.

  • Adkins Scholarship Foundation — For Guam high school juniors and seniors based on academic merit; deadline: Spring (TBA). Learn more
  • Bank of Guam Ifit Scholarship — For Guam high school seniors planning to attend a 4-year college; deadline: April 2026. Learn more
  • CoreTech Foundation Scholarship — Awards for selected graduating high school seniors from Guam pursuing a U.S.-accredited four-year college or university; eligibility: graduating Guam high school seniors; deadline: April 17, 2026 (confirm the next annual cycle). Learn more
  • Father Duenas Memorial School Scholarships — Merit and need-based awards for FD students; eligibility: FDMS students; deadline: Varies. Learn more
  • GCA Trades Academy Scholarship — Tuition support for training at GCA Trades Academy; eligibility: Guam residents who meet the current application requirements, including being age 18 or older for the 2026 scholarship cycle; application window: announced annually in spring. Learn more
  • Guam Merit Scholarship — Postsecondary scholarship for top graduating Guam public, private, or DoDEA high school students; eligibility: qualifying Guam high school graduates with a 3.0+ GPA and required citizenship/residency qualifications; application window: confirm the current annual cycle with GDOE/UOG. Learn more
  • Guam Society of America Scholarship — For students with Guam heritage or ties; eligibility: Guam residents/descendants; deadline: May 2026. Learn more
  • JFK High School Scholarship Foundation — Specific awards for students graduating from JFK High School; eligibility: JFK High School seniors; deadline: January 10, 2026. Learn more
  • Junior Achievement of Guam Scholarship — College scholarships for qualified graduating seniors who participated in Junior Achievement during high school; eligibility: Guam high school seniors with JA participation and a 3.0+ GPA; application window: annual—confirm the current cycle with JA Guam. Learn more
  • Moda Gino’s Partners in Education Scholarship — Textbook and education-cost support for Guam public-school seniors; eligibility: graduating Guam public high school seniors who meet residency, GPA, and full-time enrollment requirements for UOG, GCC, or GCA Trades Academy; application window: annual—confirm the next cycle with Moda Gino’s. Learn more
  • SAME Guam Post Charlie Corn Scholarship — One-year support for engineering or architecture study; eligibility: current high school seniors who are permanent residents of Guam, CNMI, FSM, or Palau (or qualifying military dependents) and who plan to enroll in an accredited engineering or architecture program; application window: SY 2026–27 application is open—see the current Guam Post application for deadline. Learn more
  • Shieh Su Ying Scholar Athlete Program — Scholarship recognition for graduating student athletes who demonstrate academics, sports, service, and leadership; eligibility: graduating Guam high school seniors who meet the published academic and athletic criteria; application window: annual spring cycle. Learn more
  • St. John’s School Scholarships — Academic and need-based scholarships for St. John’s students; eligibility: St. John’s students; deadline: March 31. Learn more
  • United for the Culture (U4C) Guam Scholarship — Community scholarship supporting educational goals; eligibility: graduating high school seniors in Guam, subject to the official 2026 application requirements; application window: announced by U4C Guam. Learn more

Learn more
